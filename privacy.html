Privacy Policy

Woows Studio Uploader · Last updated: November 7, 2026

Woows Studio Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Woows Studio. It is used only by the Woows Studio team to publish videos to YouTube channels and TikTok accounts owned by Woows Studio. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel to the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

The app uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. TikTok's handling of your data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy and use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube channel ID and name of the connected channel (youtube.readonly), used to confirm which channel the videos are uploaded to.
  • Google OAuth refresh token, used to upload approved videos (youtube.upload), add them to the channel's playlists (youtube) and upload their English captions (youtube.force-ssl).
  • TikTok open ID and display name of the connected account (user.info.basic), used to confirm which account receives the drafts.
  • TikTok access and refresh tokens, used to send approved videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload).
  • Upload results (video ID, upload time, publish time, draft delivery status) for videos the app itself sent.

The app does not read viewer data, comments, followers, subscribers, analytics, messages, email, contacts or any other Google or TikTok account data.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The data is used only to publish Woows Studio's own videos and to show their status to the Woows Studio team.
  • It is stored on Woows Studio's own computer. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • When a channel or account is disconnected in the app, its stored tokens are deleted.
  • You can revoke the app's Google access at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party apps, and its TikTok access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the channel or account.
  • To request deletion of any remaining stored data, email the contact address below. We delete it within 30 days.

Cookies

The app's own dashboard uses a single login cookie for its team members. This website does not use cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
