Woows Studio Uploader

Woows Studio Uploader is an internal publishing tool operated by Woows Studio. It is used only by the Woows Studio team to publish videos to YouTube channels and TikTok accounts that Woows Studio owns (for example Mind Trick Lab and Whispered Legend).

What the app does

  • Produces original videos (puzzles, science facts, illustrated folklore stories) for Woows Studio's own channels.
  • A team member reviews and approves every video before it is published.
  • Uploads approved videos to the connected Woows Studio YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, with a scheduled publish time, and adds the script's English captions and the channel's playlists.
  • Sends short vertical cuts of approved videos to the connected Woows Studio TikTok account as drafts. A team member opens the TikTok inbox notification, reviews the draft and posts it in the TikTok app.

Google / YouTube access

  • youtube.upload: to upload approved videos to the connected channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's ID and name so the right channel is used.
  • youtube: to create the channel's playlists and add uploaded videos to them.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to upload English caption files for videos the app uploaded.

TikTok access

  • user.info.basic: to read the connected account's display name so the right account is used.
  • video.upload: to send approved videos to the connected account's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted until a team member completes the post in TikTok.

The app is not offered to the public and does not access any other Google or TikTok account data.
